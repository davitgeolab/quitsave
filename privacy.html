Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2, 2026
In short: Quit & Save does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The habits you track (for example, how many cigarettes or cups of coffee you used per day and what they cost), your start dates, your saving goal and your settings are saved only on your device. We have no servers and no account system, so this information never leaves your iPhone and we cannot see it.

If you use iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), this data may be included in your backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No selling or sharing of any data — there is nothing to share.

Notifications

Reminders and milestone alerts are local notifications. They are scheduled and shown by your iPhone itself — no server is involved and nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Premium is active on your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
